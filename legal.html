Terms and limits

CAMREXA provides research and opinions for Panama City Beach condominium associations. It is not an appraisal, home inspection, engineering report, insurance quote, or legal advice.

The free Health Check shows a risk band only. The $149 Condo Intelligence Report™ is a compiled desk file. Source documents can change after the snapshot date.

Verify current association documents before you buy, sell, or lend. Do not waive review because a CAMREXA band looks Strong.

Leads submitted on this site are used to deliver the Health Check, the paid report if requested, and HOA Watch updates. They are not sold.

If a Meta or Google tag ID is installed, we use it to measure Check My Condo, Health Check views, $149 checkout starts, and Watch signups, and to show CAMREXA ads to people who already visited. You can use browser controls to limit ad cookies.

CAMREXA is separate from Ameritech Roofing and Miller & Associates Realty.
